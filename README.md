# Office Hours — Academic Guidance

Office Hours is a private academic support activity for Moodle courses. Learners describe learning difficulties or request guidance, and teachers respond in a private, contextual conversation, track progress, and close the case once guidance is provided.

The activity is **not an appointment-booking system**: it does not publish reservable time slots, sell or allocate places, maintain waiting lists, or provide automated scheduling. A conversation or a separately agreed meeting may be part of the teacher's response, but the core record is a learning-support case rather than a booked event.

## Learning-support workflow

1. A learner describes a question, course topic or assignment difficulty.
2. A teacher reviews the request, replies with guidance, and optionally adds staff-only notes.
3. The request moves from awaiting guidance to in progress, resolved and closed.
4. Learners may reopen a resolved or closed request if they need more help.
5. Both parties retain a private history of responses; instructors can review authorized requests within the Moodle course and group restrictions.

Requests can be used for formative feedback, individual supervision, academic advising, or assistance with course activities. The activity supports course completion based on requests submitted and resolved, rather than attendance or appointment reservations. Message notifications follow Moodle's messaging preferences.

## Access, privacy and history

Requests are private to their owner and authorized teachers; internal notes are only visible to authorized teaching staff. Moodle capabilities, course enrollment and separate-group restrictions are enforced server-side. The privacy provider covers personal data held by the new request, message and history records.

On upgraded sites, existing appointment records remain stored for archival, privacy and backup/restore compatibility. Legacy booking and availability links no longer permit creating or modifying appointments. The historical tables are not used for new requests. The previous booking scheduler is intentionally retired.

## Compatibility

Moodle 4.5 LTS and 5.x. GNU GPL v3 or later.
